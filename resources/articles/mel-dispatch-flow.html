MEL/DDP relief is not an obligation to operate. Irrespective of the provisions of the MEL, the Commander is not obliged to operate with a particular defect if, in his opinion, it could adversely affect the safety of the proposed flight. (OM-A Part A §8.6.1)

Notes
  1. A system fault that results only in a CMCS maintenance message does not require MEL/DDP consideration or maintenance action before dispatch. It is handled through the AAI standard maintenance programme.
  2. If the crew is not certain whether an alert results from the existing MEL/DDP dispatch configuration, the associated non-normal procedure should be accomplished.
  3. The AAI combined MEL/DDP uses only the (M) and (O) procedure symbols defined in the MEL/DDP Introduction.
  4. This diagram is a study aid. It does not replace the current combined MEL/DDP, OM-A or CAME. Always work from the effective revision.
