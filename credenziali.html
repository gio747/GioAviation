Behind this reference library

Fleet Captain & Line Training Captain, Boeing 747-400.

Gio holds the Fleet Captain role for the Boeing 747-400 fleet at Air Atlanta Icelandic (AAI), and is qualified as a Line Training Captain (LTC) and Type Rating Instructor (TRI). Operations span worldwide cargo routes across Europe, the Middle East, and the Far East.

Within AAI's Chief Pilot Office, Gio leads the redevelopment of the airline's Operations Manual Part C (Route and Aerodrome Instructions), working alongside Flight Ops Engineering to rebuild the manual's oceanic, route-specific, and limitation sections from primary sources — FCOM, FCTM, QRH, ICAO documentation, and EASA regulatory material.

Flight training began at Oxford Aviation College. That same discipline, zero fabrication, and a traceable source behind every stated procedure, carries directly into the documents published here.
