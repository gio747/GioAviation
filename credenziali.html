Behind this reference library

How this library is built.

Every document published here follows the same rule: nothing is stated without a verifiable source behind it. Figures, limitations and procedures are traced to the document, revision and section they come from.

Sources are primary material only: manufacturer documentation such as FCOM, FCTM and QRH, ICAO documentation and EASA regulatory material. Where a source does not cover a point, the document says so instead of filling the gap.

The material is study and reference material for training purposes. It is not approved operational data. Always refer to your operator's approved manuals and procedures.

Zero fabrication

No figure, limitation or procedure appears without a source document behind it.

Source attribution

Every reference names the manual, revision and section it was drawn from.

Dated revision

Documents carry a visible revision date and are retired when the source changes.

Independent

GioAviation.aero is independent and not affiliated with The Boeing Company or any operator.
