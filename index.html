How access works

Three steps, reviewed by the author

STEP 1

Submit a request

Tell us who you are, what you fly or study, and how you will use the material.

STEP 2

Personal review

Each request is read individually. Pilots, instructors, operators, cadets and student pilots are all welcome.

STEP 3

Credentials by email

Within 48 hours of your request, approved members receive their login credentials by email.

Your data: we ask only for what the review needs. No licence numbers and no copies of documents. Your details are used only to review your request and manage your access.
